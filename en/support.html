How do I start?

Choose New match, scoring format, players, opening serving team, server and receiver. Both opening players start in their own right service court. Tap Start match.

How do I score and undo?

Tap the team that won the rally. Use the back arrow to undo the last action, including score and service order. Multiple undo is available until you save or end the match.

What do the positions mean?

Left and right are from EACH pair’s perspective facing the net. They represent service-court order, not tactical positions during play. Even serving score means right court; odd means left. Use Correct service order if the real order differs.

Can I use it without my phone or internet?

Yes. The app runs independently on Apple Watch and saves locally after every action. Reopen it to continue your current match. No account or network connection is required for scoring.

Which formats are supported?

21 points with a 30-point cap, or 15 points with a 21-point cap; win by two before the cap. Choose one game or best of three. The selected format is fixed for the match. Verify the format used by your event.

How do I manage my data?

Swipe left on a saved match to delete it. Settings can clear all history or reset all local data, with confirmation. Reset also removes the current match. There is no developer cloud sync or export in version 1.0.

Will I lose progress?

The latest successfully saved action survives app restarts. Uninstalling the app, resetting the Watch, or losing the device may remove data. System-managed device backups are controlled by Apple. If a save fails, an alert is shown and the previous state is kept.

Is this automatic scoring or an official referee?

No. Record each rally manually. The app does not detect shuttle movement or make line, fault or umpiring decisions. It does not access HealthKit, location, microphone or contacts.

How do I change language or get a refund?

Choose System default, English, 简体中文 or 日本語 in Settings. Purchases, re-downloads and refunds are handled by Apple through your App Store account; use reportaproblem.apple.com for refund requests.
