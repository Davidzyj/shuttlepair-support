Overview

ShuttlePair is developed by Zhou Yajie. This policy applies to the Apple Watch app. The app does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics SDK, tracking or developer-operated network service.

Data stored on your device

Player names or labels you enter, match points, serving order, game results, timestamps, undo snapshots, language choice and haptic preference are stored locally on your Apple Watch. The developer does not receive them. Local backup files are used only to recover a previous valid save.

No data collection or tracking

The app does not send personal data, identifiers, usage events or diagnostics to the developer or third parties. It does not request health, location, microphone, contacts or advertising-tracking access. It has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Retention and deletion

Your current match and history remain locally until you delete them. You can delete individual matches, clear history, or reset all local data in Settings. Reset removes the current match, history, undo records, local backups and settings. Uninstalling or resetting the device may remove data. Apple-managed backups or diagnostics, if enabled by you, are governed by Apple’s policies; the developer has no access to your device backups.

Support email

If you email support, we receive your address and information you voluntarily include. We use it only to respond and investigate your request. We do not use it for marketing. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

This website and Apple services

These support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The site adds no analytics, advertising cookies or tracking scripts. GitHub may process technical request information such as IP addresses under its own privacy statement. App Store purchase and payment information is handled by Apple; the app does not receive your payment-card details.

Children, changes and contact

The app has no special data collection for children. If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. Questions or privacy requests: jay212315@gmail.com.
